# PS-2026-060 — Bounded experiment proposals

## Purpose

Establish the durable safety contract for Commander-controlled local self-improvement experiments without granting experiment execution or configuration activation.

## Changes

- Added authenticated GET/POST experiment-proposal APIs backed by a dedicated durable store.
- Proposals require an evidenced planning advisory; distinct champion/challenger IDs; hypothesis; predefined metrics and success/failure thresholds; evidence requirements; bounded run/time limits; local scope; rollback; and distinct known preparation/evaluator roles.
- Run count is limited to 2–20, duration to at most 24 hours, permitted external cost to exactly $0, and environment to local with no external side effects.
- Stable IDs are immutable and idempotent.
- Every proposal is protected and `review_required`, with execution, objective creation, scheduling, activation, and configuration change explicitly false.

## Safety

- Proposal creation cannot approve or execute an experiment, create work, schedule/retry/rerun, spend, act externally, or change configuration.
- Experiment-run approval remains separate from any later configuration-activation decision.
- Existing Commander approval, immutable audit history, E-stop, credential, financial, publication, external-action, and evidence gates remain authoritative.
