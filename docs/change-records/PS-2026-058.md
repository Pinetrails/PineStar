# PS-2026-058 — Morning Brief experiment-planning projection

## Purpose

Make existing experiment-planning advisories visible in the deterministic Morning Brief without turning reporting into an experiment or configuration control surface.

## Changes

- Added a bounded `experimentAdvisories` shared-report field for advisory identity, question, design requirements, and source references.
- Morning Brief projects up to ten in-period experiment-planning advisory reports into that dedicated field.
- Advisory and evaluation-report provenance enter the bounded Morning Brief evidence references.
- Existing decisions, next actions, completion signals, and their caps remain unchanged.

## Verification

- Morning Brief projection and persistence: PASS (29 assertions).
- Experiment-planning advisory regression: PASS (18 assertions).
- Champion/challenger regression: PASS (20 assertions).
- Relevant syntax checks passed.
- `git diff --check` passed apart from line-ending notices.

## Safety

- Projection is read-only and creates or edits no advisory or objective.
- It cannot select a configuration or cohort, schedule or rerun an experiment, or decide/admit/activate/retire/mutate configuration.
- Existing Commander approval, immutable audit/report history, E-stop, credential, financial, publication, external-action, and evidence gates remain authoritative.
