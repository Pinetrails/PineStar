# PS-2026-057 — Evaluation-informed experiment-planning advisory

## Purpose

Use retained champion/challenger lessons to prepare a bounded question and evidence-informed requirements for a possible future experiment without selecting a configuration, defining executable cohorts, or scheduling work.

## Changes

- Added authenticated `POST /api/evolution/experiment-planning-advisories`.
- The immutable shared-report advisory requires explicit champion/challenger report provenance and at least one retained structured lesson.
- Unique lesson guidance becomes bounded design requirements while the original structured lessons remain attached.
- Stable advisory IDs are idempotent and reject changed content.
- Configuration, candidate, cohort, schedule, activation, admission, retirement, and rerun-shaped inputs are refused.
