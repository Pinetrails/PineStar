# PS-2026-033 — Read-Only Product Contribution UI

**Date:** 2026-09-02
**Status:** Complete

## Intent

Make existing evidence-based product contribution visible to the user without turning Reports into a financial-action surface or overstating incomplete data.

## Implementation

- Extended the existing portfolio renderer to accept the period financial summary already returned by the authenticated ledger read.
- Shows recorded net contribution and evidence-entry counts for known product IDs.
- Excludes unmatched product IDs from portfolio claims.
- Shows an explicit no-evidence state and a visible warning that missing costs are not estimated.

## Safety and truthfulness

- The view says “recorded product contribution,” not profit, profitability, or ROI.
- It adds no form, button, external link, payment, refund, purchase, publication, approval, execution, sync, account, credential, or spending path.
- Existing authentication, evidence, audit, cancellation, E-stop, and protected-action boundaries are unchanged.
