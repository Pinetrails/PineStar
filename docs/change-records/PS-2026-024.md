# PS-2026-024 — Truthful Commerce & Business Records

**Date:** 2026-08-30
**Status:** Complete

## Intent

Record project-linked commerce state and evidenced revenue, expense, and refund facts without granting Pine Star authority over marketplaces, accounts, payments, or spending.

## Implementation

- Added a crash-safe station business store with bounded commerce and financial ledgers.
- Commerce records require an existing product project and marketplace. `observed_published` requires explicit evidence and remains an observation, not a publication operation.
- Financial entries require a stable type, non-negative USD amount, source, occurrence time, and evidence references. Optional product links must resolve to existing projects.
- Stable IDs are idempotent; changed facts conflict rather than rewriting the original observation.
- Added authenticated create/list/filter APIs for commerce references and the business ledger, including evidenced period totals.
- Added business period totals and source references to deterministic Morning Brief composition.

## Safety

- Every record explicitly carries `recordsExternalAction:false` and `spendingAuthorityUsd:0`.
- No marketplace adapter, publication operation, payment/refund action, automatic upgrade, account creation, credential field, outreach, subscription, purchase, or approval shortcut was added.
- Financial entries are records of known external facts, not estimates or instructions to transact.
