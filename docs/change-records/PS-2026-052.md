**Date:** 2026-09-06

## Intent

Create a durable configuration-candidate record after advisory evaluation, before considering any admission mechanism, while preserving Commander authority and reversible experimentation evidence.

## Results

- Added authenticated `GET` and `POST /api/evolution/configuration-candidates`.
- Candidate creation requires an existing immutable `champion-challenger-evaluation` report whose decision recommends the challenger.
- Records retain stable champion/challenger configuration identity, system role, evaluation provenance, intended changes, risks, rollback plan, and evidence references.
- Stable retries are idempotent and changed facts conflict; candidates remain immutable and read-only after creation.
