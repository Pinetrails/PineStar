# PS-2026-026 — Pine Trail Printables Workflow Preset

**Date:** 2026-08-31
**Status:** Complete

## Intent

Provide a reusable Pine Trail Printables intake contract over Pine Star's existing product workflow, with original distributable assets and practical print requirements, without duplicating projects, objectives, reports, execution, or commerce controls.

## Implementation

- Added supported printable families: planner, checklist, tracker, activity sheet, and bundle.
- Added stable `pine-trail-*` product identities and bounded title, customer, use-case, marketplace-reference, assumption, and asset fields.
- Standardized original Pine Trail-owned visual assets, print-safe margins/contrast, editable source plus export-ready PDF, and US Letter/A4 layouts.
- Delegated normalized intake to the existing Product Idea workflow, which creates/reuses the product project, Coordinator parent, Researcher validation, dependent Idea Lab brief, and shared report.
- Added authenticated `POST /api/product-projects/pine-trail-printables`.

## Safety

- The preset performs no publishing, listing, marketplace mutation, message, outreach, account creation, purchase, subscription, credential access, or spending.
- Responses explicitly carry `externalAction:false` and `spendingAuthorityUsd:0`.
- Existing objective admission, activation, cancellation, Night Shift/E-stop, settlement evidence, report, and audit behavior is unchanged.
- Original-asset requirements reinforce the existing release rule against distributing StarNet or copyrighted commercial-game art.
