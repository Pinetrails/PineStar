# PS-2026-025 — Bounded Growth Experiments

**Date:** 2026-08-31
**Status:** Complete

## Intent

Let Pine Star plan and analyze small product-growth hypotheses through existing objectives and shared reports without granting authority to advertise, contact users, publish, create accounts, spend, or scale a result automatically.

## Implementation

- Added an economy-tier `business.growth_analyst` system role with experiment-design and analysis capabilities and explicit zero publish/outreach/spend/account permissions.
- Added stable growth plan normalization with a product, hypothesis, named metric, direction, numeric baseline/target, method, and evidence references.
- Plans are available only for listing-ready or later products, create one directly assigned Growth Analyst objective, and link one shared plan report to the product.
- Results require the matching linked Growth Analyst objective to be completed plus an observed value, positive sample size, bounded outcome, and explicit evidence.
- Plan and result retries are idempotent; changed facts under the same stable identity conflict after normalized report comparison.
- Evidenced result decisions, next actions, and report provenance enter deterministic Business Morning Brief composition.
- Added authenticated plan and result endpoints.

## Safety

- Both workflow responses carry `externalAction:false` and `spendingAuthorityUsd:0`.
- Objective instructions forbid publication, advertising, outreach, messages, account creation, purchases, subscriptions, and spending.
- A supported result is only a recorded observation. It does not scale, deploy, advertise, publish, or approve another action.
- Normal authentication, objective cancellation/E-stop, runtime admission, settlement evidence, report, and audit controls remain authoritative.
