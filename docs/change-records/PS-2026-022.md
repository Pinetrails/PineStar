# PS-2026-022 — Product Production & QA Planning

**Date:** 2026-08-30
**Status:** Complete

## Intent

Turn a planned product into bounded specification, real-deliverable preparation, and independent QA work without duplicating existing artifact/file trust or granting listing/publication authority.

## Implementation

- Added Product Designer and Quality Reviewer system roles with explicit internal capabilities and no publication/spending authority.
- Added normalized stable production plans with bounded specification seed, deliverables, constraints, and QA checklist.
- Coordinator owns a three-child dependency graph: specification → product preparation → independent quality review.
- Product preparation explicitly uses existing trusted workspace/Workshop paths and must cite real artifact evidence; the business record stores expectations, not files or claims.
- Added one stable shared production-plan report, project/objective/report links, `planned → production`, and authenticated `POST /api/product-projects/production-plan`.

## Safety

- Planning does not admit, activate, queue, write artifacts, list, publish, message, purchase, subscribe, create accounts, access credentials, or grant spending authority.
- Existing routing, dependencies, admission, activation, Workshop/file trust, provenance, cancellation, E-stop, settlement, audit, authentication, and approvals remain authoritative.
