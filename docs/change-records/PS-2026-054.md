# PS-2026-054 — Evaluation Evidence-Quality Gate

**Date:** 2026-09-06
**Status:** Complete

## Intent

Prevent incomplete or weak champion/challenger measurements from becoming configuration candidates while preserving all results as advisory evidence.

## Results

- A recommendation now requires at least two settled objectives in each arm, one matched measured run per objective, and completion evidence for every completed objective.
- Comparisons with uncertain mutations, small cohorts, missing runs, or unevidenced completions are `inconclusive` and report the specific limitation.
- Complete evaluations carry `evaluation-evidence:complete-v1` provenance.
- New configuration candidates require that marker in addition to a challenger recommendation; older or incomplete reports remain readable history but cannot seed a candidate.
