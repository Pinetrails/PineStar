# PS-2026-032 — Evidence-Based Product Contribution

**Date:** 2026-09-02
**Status:** Complete

## Intent

Connect existing evidence-backed financial facts to product portfolio decisions without estimating missing data or adding financial authority.

## Implementation

- Extended existing period summaries with deterministic per-project revenue, expense, refund, net, and entry counts.
- Kept unallocated ledger entry counts explicit rather than assigning them speculatively.
- Extended the existing product-portfolio projection with the number of known products carrying ledger evidence and their recorded net contribution.
- Added negative recorded product contribution to portfolio attention items.
- Fed the same period summary into the product portfolio used by Business Morning Brief and retained ledger source references.

## Safety and truthfulness

- Only immutable ledger rows with explicit product-project IDs are attributed.
- Financial rows for unknown products do not become product claims.
- Missing costs remain missing; the wording is “recorded contribution,” not profit, profitability, or ROI.
- No payment, refund, purchase, subscription, budget, spending, marketplace, publication, account, credential, or external-write capability was added.
- Authentication, approvals, E-stop, cancellation, audit, and evidence requirements are unchanged.
