# Upstream StarNet policy

StarNet is Pine Star's upstream technical foundation. `origin` is the Pine Star fork and `upstream` points to `androoAGI/starnet`. The permanent comparison point is `starnet-baseline-0.10.10` at `56c3848e`.

Never modify, move, retag, recreate, or delete that tag. Never blindly merge upstream.

## Review workflow

1. Fetch/review only when authorized; fetching does not authorize merge/push.
2. Identify the upstream change and Pine Star need.
3. Review code, migrations, tests, licensing, assets, branding, security, privacy, providers, and release effects.
4. Prefer a focused cherry-pick or deliberate adaptation.
5. Test affected Pine Star paths and compare with baseline when useful.
6. Record the upstream reference, Change ID, decision, and result.

Pine Star current-authority decisions win for identity, art, autonomy, memory, business boundaries, and roadmap. Preserve compatible StarNet technical laws. Record conflicts rather than silently resolving them.

StarNet's private signing key is unavailable and inappropriate for Pine Star. Pine Star will establish an independent signing/release process.
