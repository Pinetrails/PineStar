# Pine Star vision

## Current purpose

Pine Star is a desktop multi-agent AI workspace built from StarNet's open-source architecture. Its foundation principle is: **preserve working architecture first; transform gradually; do not rewrite working systems without a good reason.**

The near-term goal is a dependable, inspectable workspace where a user can coordinate a team or speak directly to a specialist. Windows is the primary development platform during foundation work.

## Planned product character

Pine Star should feel less like a command dashboard and more like a living AI workplace. Work should be visible, organized, attributable, cost-aware, and reviewable. Conventional panels remain appropriate for settings, files, providers, credentials, budgets, logs, and technical administration.

The central rule is: **do the work at the lowest capable level; escalate only when needed.** A configurable coordinator can organize work without becoming a mandatory personality bottleneck.

## Long-term experience

The future home screen is a top-down explorable workplace with a Game Boy Advance-era RPG visual language and entirely original assets. Buildings/areas represent teams, tools, projects, or functions; agents visibly occupy spaces associated with their work. Clock-based day/night behavior conveys the passage of work without pretending animation proves activity.

This is future direction, not an implemented feature. Functional foundations, truthful state, safety, and reliable operations come first. See [ART_DIRECTION.md](ART_DIRECTION.md) and [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md).

## Sustainable usefulness

Pine Star's economic objective is **useful output + sustainable net income - unnecessary cost**. This is a responsible-use constraint, not permission for reckless monetization. See [BUSINESS_SYSTEM.md](BUSINESS_SYSTEM.md).
