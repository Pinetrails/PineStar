# StarNet v0.5.2

Your agents now ask before they guess — and finished work finally has a front door.

## New

- **Task Briefs.** Before consequential work starts, your agent settles a short brief —
  and when a decision genuinely matters, it asks ONE concrete question with 2–3 options
  and a starred recommendation (with the reason). Answers survive restarts, work on
  Telegram/Discord/Slack/Matrix/Signal too, and a question never burns the run: it ends
  cleanly as a decision turn, not a cancellation.
- **Recipes declare their decisions.** 29 recipes now surface their material choice at
  launch (tone, format, depth…) as a one-tap intake — answer up front instead of getting
  interrupted mid-run.
- **The OUTBOX opens.** Clicking the OUTBOX now opens a clean finished-work window:
  read the run's transcript, review the digest, and jump to the task — away-work is no
  longer a crate you can't see into.
- **Deliveries land in their own session.** Workshop builds arrive as their own unread
  session with the full return card, so nothing lands silently.
- **Session search on the rail.** Search across every session title and transcript right
  under the SESSIONS list — a hit opens its session. Export any conversation (.md/.json)
  from its row menu; secrets and hidden data are always excluded.
- **Session starters.** Empty sessions offer context-aware opening chips — your usual
  recipe, something new to try, and your agent's pitch.
- **6 new skins.** Beach Babe, Secret Agent, Grim Reaper, Plague Doctor, and two founder
  homages join the roster.

## Fixed

- Deleted sessions stay deleted — no more resurrection after restart (same for forgotten
  memories, dossier entries, and removed automations).
- A busy agent now says so in every session — "BUSY IN <session>" with a jump to the
  active run, instead of silently eating your message.
- If the station dies mid-reply, the partial answer is kept and the error tells you what
  actually happened ("just now" also no longer reads as "1 min ago").
- Stopped runs offer **Try again**. Rapid +NEW no longer piles up blank sessions.
- Recovery export **never** includes provider keys or channel secrets — and says so.
- Disabled connectors survive restarts as manageable rows; local providers (Ollama,
  custom endpoints) show truthful LOCAL ENDPOINT status instead of a fake "KEY SAVED".
- Windows can't be dragged/resized off-screen anymore; positions repair themselves.
- Night Shift focus only accepts real, trusted projects — and CLEAR actually clears.
- Camera and fullscreen motion smoothed; room lighting no longer bleeds into space.
