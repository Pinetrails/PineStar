# StarNet v0.6.3

The onboarding your answers actually reach.

## Fixed
- **Awakening answers are never lost.** Typing an answer into the message box during the
  first-meeting interview now lands on the question on screen — previously those answers were
  silently discarded, leaving your overseer knowing nothing about you no matter how much you
  told it. Everything you say now becomes real, permanent context (visible in the COMMANDER
  dossier).
- **Typing can't misfire the interview.** Number keys typed into a text box no longer click
  interview options, and Enter no longer jumps the flow mid-sentence.
- **Window maximize/restore glyph** renders crisply at every display scale (inline SVG,
  replacing the old bar art that aliased under fractional scaling).

## Changed
- **Every onboarding question rewritten for clarity.** The awakening and the get-to-know-you
  questions are now plain, direct, and answerable in one breath — no metaphors, no riddles.
  Clearer choices ("I run my own business", "I work a job", "I create / build my own projects",
  "I'm a student") with follow-ups that ask exactly for what the station needs to know.
- **Personality presets trimmed to seven voices.** OVERLORD, GREMLIN, and NOIR are retired;
  existing agents using them map to the closest remaining voice automatically.
